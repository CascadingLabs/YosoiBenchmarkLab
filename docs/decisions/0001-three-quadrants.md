# Decision 0001: three required quadrants

Status: accepted.

The Benchmark Lab has exactly three required quadrants:

1. parser and selector engines;
2. HTTP scraping frameworks;
3. rendered browser Requests.

They run in that order, and the required program stops after the third.

Self-hosted product suites and adaptive/AI algorithms may become optional
appendices, but they do not block publication. Hosted APIs and browser
automation are out of scope.
